# Research Origin & Inception Statement

MIR Medical Intelligence is a research program initiated by Mir AbdulRehman and subsequently developed as an interdisciplinary collaborative research project with his Biomedical Co-Researcher.

The project combines software engineering, machine learning, biomedical research, and clinical-intelligence methodology. This record documents the evolution of the research concept, contributions, versions, and external collaborations.

It does not by itself determine legal ownership or formal academic credit; those matters remain subject to applicable agreements, institutional policies, dataset licenses / data-use agreements (DUAs), and journal publishing standards.
