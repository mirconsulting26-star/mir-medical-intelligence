# Risk Management & Clinical Safety Plan

## 1. Intent & Scope
MIR Medical Intelligence is a research platform. It does NOT make automated diagnostic decisions or replace licensed clinicians.

## 2. Risk Mitigation Protocols
- **Data Coverage Indicator**: Every model output must report feature completeness (e.g. "Coverage: 60% of baseline panel available").
- **Uncertainty Quantification**: Predictions must include 95% confidence intervals (bootstrapped or conformal prediction).
- **Non-Diagnostic Language**: Report phrasings must state: *"Pattern identified is non-specific and represents a statistical risk signal for clinician review."*
