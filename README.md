# MIR Medical Intelligence

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Proposition
MIR Medical Intelligence (MIR-MI) is an open research platform investigating how routine, non-imaging clinical laboratory data and longitudinal EHR records can support:
1. **Physiological Anomaly Detection**: Identifying clinically meaningful deviations in standard lab panels (e.g., Complete Blood Count - CBC, metabolic panels).
2. **Longitudinal Trajectory Characterization**: Tracking temporal drift, volatility, and early signs of acute deterioration or chronic progression.
3. **Disease-Specific Outcome Prediction**: Modeling risk across cardiovascular, renal, metabolic, inflammatory, hepatic, hematologic, and oncologic conditions.
4. **Adaptive Test Selection & Evidence Acquisition**: Uncertainty-driven inquiry that requests high-value missing tests when materially beneficial, rather than demanding static inputs.

---

## 2. Research Tracks Overview
- **Track A**: Data Harmonization & Physiological Anomaly Detection (NHANES, MIMIC-IV)
- **Track B**: Disease-Specific Benchmark Prediction Models (Classical ML & Boosting)
- **Track C**: Temporal, Sequential & Survival Modeling (Deep Learning & Recurrent Architectures)
- **Track D**: External Validation & Distributional Shift Evaluation (eICU, UK Biobank)
- **Track E**: Federated Laboratory Intelligence (Privacy-Preserving Multi-Site Learning via Flower/NVIDIA FLARE)
- **Track F**: Constrained Agentic Clinical Orchestration & Multi-Modal Future Expansion
