# MIR Medical Intelligence Core Research Protocol (v2.2)

## 1. Primary Objectives
1. **Physiological Anomaly Detection**: Evaluate unsupervised (Isolation Forests, Autoencoders, Mahalanobis distance) and supervised detection of laboratory anomalies in routine CBC and metabolic panels.
2. **Missingness-Aware Prediction**: Compare missingness indicators, pattern masks, and native tree-based missingness handling against standard imputation.
3. **Adaptive Test Selection**: Develop expected-information-value policies that recommend high-yield supplementary laboratory tests.
4. **Federated Privacy Protection**: Demonstrate multi-site laboratory model training across simulated hospital partitions using Flower / NVIDIA FLARE.
