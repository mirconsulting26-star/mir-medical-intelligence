# Dataset Inventory & Card Registry

## 1. NHANES (National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey)
- **Source**: CDC / NCHS
- **Tier**: Public Open Access
- **Content**: Broad US population survey, CBC, biochemistry, survey weights.
- **Use Case**: Track A baseline laboratory anomaly detection & population normality.

## 2. MIMIC-IV (Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care)
- **Source**: PhysioNet / MIT Lab for Computational Physiology
- **Tier**: Credentialed Tier 2 (DUA Required)
- **Content**: ICU/ED longitudinal laboratory measurements, vital signs, outcomes.
- **Use Case**: Track B & C longitudinal trajectories & survival outcomes.
