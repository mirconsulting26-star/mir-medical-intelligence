## 1. Executive Summary of Data 2021-2023
After suspension of NHANES field operations in March 2020 due to the COVID-19 pandemic, data collection began for a new 2-year cycle in August 2021. The August 2021-August 2023 nationally representative data files are based on an updated sample design and modified questionnaires, and examination procedures.
