# Regulatory Considerations & Compliance Notes

## 1. European Union Medical Device Regulation (EU MDR)
- Software intended to provide information for diagnostic or therapeutic decisions falls under **MDR Rule 11**.
- Research software used strictly for scientific validation and non-clinical experimentation is exempt from CE marking requirements until formal clinical trial or commercial distribution intent is initiated.

## 2. EU Artificial Intelligence Act (EU AI Act)
- AI systems used in healthcare decision support are categorized as **High-Risk AI Systems**.
- Requirements: Technical documentation, risk management (ISO 14971), human oversight, data governance, post-market monitoring.
