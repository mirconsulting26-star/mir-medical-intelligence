# Research Governance & Decision-Making Protocol

## 1. Governance Principles
MIR Medical Intelligence is governed as an interdisciplinary research collaboration between Mir AbdulRehman (Technical / Research Lead) and the Biomedical Co-Researcher.

### Key Rules:
1. **Equal Methodological Oversight**: Technical ML decisions and clinical interpretation require mutual review before study release.
2. **AI Assistance Transparency**: AI tools (e.g. LLMs) are treated as research instruments, not authors. All AI usage must be logged, verified, and attributed according to ICMJE rules.
3. **No Fabricated Credentials**: Educational background and prior publications are stated accurately without distortion.
4. **Reproducibility Guarantee**: Every benchmark, cohort definition, and figure must be programmatically reproducible via Git commit, seed, and environment hash.
